About Marquee

We started Marquee with a simple complaint: most menswear is either disposable or absurdly priced. So we work directly with a handful of mills and small workshops, keep the range small, and put the budget into fabric and construction instead of packaging.

Every piece is designed to move between the office and the evening without changing out of it.
